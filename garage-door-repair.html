Door Troubleshooting

When Your Door Isn't Operating Correctly

Garage and overhead door problems are not always caused by a single component. A door may stop operating correctly because of mechanical wear, damaged hardware, alignment problems, opener or operator issues, or several conditions occurring together.

David's Overhead Door evaluates the overall door system rather than simply replacing parts without identifying the cause of the problem.

  • Door will not open or close
  • Door is crooked or uneven
  • Door comes off track
  • Door is unusually loud
  • Door binds or sticks during operation
  • Opener or operator does not respond
  • Door closes improperly
  • Visible damaged or worn hardware
